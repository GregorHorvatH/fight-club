# Soundtrack prompts

One soundtrack for every fighter, written from how the fighter looks. Each prompt is what one would give to a music generator (Suno and the like); the game itself plays a synthesised version written from the same prompt (`src/audio/styles.ts` and `src/audio/composer.ts`; James's by hand in `src/audio/fightTheme.ts`), so nothing here has to be generated to play the game.

In a fight the soundtrack of the **opponent** plays (against the machine, in the tournament and in the arcade); between two players, one of the two by chance. In **Options → Soundtrack** one can choose any soundtrack of a fighter that is unlocked, or leave it on the opponent's.
